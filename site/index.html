About us

We build what we needed first.

Forge Vault Holdings started from a simple frustration: the tools we wanted either didn't exist, or existed badly. So we built them for ourselves — and then published them for everyone else with the same problem.

BioPrint began as a way to track our own protocols — bloodwork, dosing, training, sleep — because nothing on the market connected those dots. IgniteRegen came from wanting a research supply catalog where the certificate of analysis mattered more than the marketing copy.

Every product we own started the same way: a problem we actually had, solved properly, then opened up. That order matters. We are our own first users, so the things that would annoy you have already annoyed us.

We own and operate what we build. We don't flip companies, and we don't ship anything we wouldn't run ourselves.
